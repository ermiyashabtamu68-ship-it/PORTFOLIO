01 / ABOUT

Curious about how things work.

I'm from Harar, Ethiopia, and currently a student at Bethlehem Catholic School, where I'm part of the STEM Program run in partnership with the Menschen für Menschen Foundation. That program is where a lot of my hands-on work happens — building electronics, writing firmware, and turning classroom ideas into working prototypes with my classmates.

I design, build and program practical technology — combining physical systems like electronics, sensors, motors and machines with software, websites and mobile apps. My work spans agricultural automation, robotics, and educational and fitness apps, and I like projects that solve a real, local problem rather than just checking a box.

Smart Farm — ESP32 Irrigation System

A low-cost ESP32-based smart irrigation system with soil moisture, temperature and humidity sensing, an LCD status display, and dual remote control: a Blynk Wi-Fi dashboard plus a GSM (SIM800L) missed-call trigger for areas with unreliable internet. Built with Abenezer Ayalew and Boni Zerihun, advised by Mr. Abdulaziz, for the Bethlehem Catholic School STEM Program in partnership with Menschen für Menschen.
